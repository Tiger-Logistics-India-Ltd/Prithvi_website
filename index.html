Kothali Spiritual Centre

Nestled in the serene surroundings of Kothali Village, the Prithvi Foundation's spiritual centre serves as a place of devotion, community service, and collective well-being. Rooted in the spirit of seva and spiritual harmony, the centre brings together people from nearby villages and visiting devotees through a range of spiritual, cultural, and community-focused activities.

At the heart of the centre's work is the belief that spiritual spaces should also nurture the physical, social, and emotional well-being of the communities around them. Regular yoga and meditation workshops, havan and pooja ceremonies, and community lunches are organized to serve the community.

About Prithvi Foundation

Prithvi Foundation is a non-governmental organization dedicated to serving society through spiritual, humanitarian, and community-focused initiatives. Founded in 2018 and registered under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882, the Foundation is based in Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand.
