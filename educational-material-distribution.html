Educational Material Distribution

Prithvi Foundation believes that education is the most powerful tool for social transformation. Through its Educational Material Distribution programme, the Foundation provides essential school supplies — including notebooks, pens, pencils, erasers, geometry sets, and other stationery — to children from underprivileged families in Tehri Garhwal and surrounding villages.

Many families in these remote Himalayan regions face financial challenges that make it difficult to afford basic school materials. By ensuring that every child has access to the tools they need for learning, Prithvi Foundation removes one of the key barriers to education in these communities.

The distribution drives are organized in coordination with local schools and community leaders, ensuring that materials reach the children who need them most. These events also serve as occasions for the Foundation to engage with families, understand their needs, and encourage continued education for their children.

Through this initiative, Prithvi Foundation reaffirms its commitment to nurturing young minds and supporting the educational aspirations of the next generation in Uttarakhand.
