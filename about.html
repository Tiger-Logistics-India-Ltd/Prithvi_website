About Us

▼ About Prithvi Foundation

“Seva Paramo Dharmaḥ” — Service is the highest duty.

Prithvi Foundation is a non-governmental organization dedicated to serving society through spiritual, humanitarian, and community-focused initiatives. Founded in 2018 and registered under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882, the Foundation is based in Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand, a region deeply rooted in India's spiritual and ecological heritage.

Guided by the values of service, compassion, and harmony with nature, Prithvi Foundation works to uplift the lives of local communities while preserving the spiritual traditions that have long defined the Himalayan region. The Foundation believes that sustainable social development must go hand in hand with cultural and spiritual well-being.

Through its various initiatives, Prithvi Foundation supports local community development, pilgrimage services, and charitable outreach. At its spiritual centre, the Foundation regularly organizes numerous charitable and spiritual programs that bring together devotees, volunteers, and visitors in a shared spirit of service and learning.

Its work includes efforts aimed at improving livelihoods in surrounding communities, supporting pilgrims who travel through the region, and creating spaces for spiritual reflection, service, and collective welfare.

Rooted in the timeless ethos of seva (selfless service), Prithvi Foundation continues to work towards nurturing a more compassionate, spiritually aware, and socially responsible society.
