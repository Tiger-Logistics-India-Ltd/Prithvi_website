Yoga & Meditation

Yoga and Meditation are integral to the holistic well-being programmes offered at the Prithvi Spiritual Centre. Set amidst the serene natural beauty of the Himalayan foothills, the centre provides an ideal setting for the practice of yoga, pranayama, and meditation — drawing from India's ancient traditions of mind-body harmony.

Regular yoga sessions are conducted both in the centre's dedicated indoor meditation room and in the open-air meditation area, allowing participants to connect with nature while practising asanas and breathing exercises. These sessions are open to people of all ages and experience levels, from beginners to advanced practitioners.

The meditation workshops focus on cultivating mindfulness, inner peace, and emotional balance. Guided by experienced instructors, participants learn techniques for calming the mind, reducing stress, and deepening their awareness — skills that benefit every aspect of daily life.

Through these programmes, Prithvi Foundation seeks to make the transformative benefits of yoga and meditation accessible to rural communities, promoting physical health, mental clarity, and spiritual growth among all who participate.
