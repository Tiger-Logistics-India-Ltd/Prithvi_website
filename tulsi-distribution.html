Herbal Plantation Drive

The Tulsi (Holy Basil) plant holds a place of deep reverence in Indian culture and spirituality. Regarded as a sacred plant with powerful medicinal properties, Tulsi has been cultivated in Indian households for centuries as a symbol of purity, devotion, and well-being. Prithvi Foundation honours this tradition through its Herbal Plantation Drive programme.

Through this initiative, the Foundation distributes Tulsi saplings to families in surrounding villages, encouraging every household to nurture this sacred plant. The distribution drives are accompanied by awareness about the spiritual significance and health benefits of Tulsi — from its role in traditional Ayurvedic medicine to its importance in daily worship and environmental health.

Tulsi is known for its antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and immune-boosting properties, making it a valuable addition to every home. By promoting its cultivation, Prithvi Foundation combines spiritual tradition with practical health and environmental consciousness.

The Herbal Plantation Drive programme reflects the Foundation's belief that small, meaningful acts — rooted in tradition and care for nature — can create lasting positive change in the lives of communities across Uttarakhand.
