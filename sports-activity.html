Sports Activity

Prithvi Foundation recognizes that physical fitness and recreation are essential components of holistic development. Through its Sports Activity programme, the Foundation organizes regular sports events and recreational activities for children and youth from Kothali and surrounding villages.

These activities provide an opportunity for young people to engage in healthy competition, develop teamwork skills, and build physical strength and endurance. From outdoor games and athletic events to group exercises, the sports programme is designed to encourage active participation and a love for physical fitness among rural youth.

In many remote Himalayan villages, children have limited access to organized sports facilities and programmes. Prithvi Foundation addresses this gap by creating spaces and occasions for sport, helping young people channel their energy positively and develop discipline, confidence, and camaraderie.

By promoting sports alongside its spiritual and educational initiatives, Prithvi Foundation takes a holistic approach to community development — nurturing the body as well as the mind and spirit.
